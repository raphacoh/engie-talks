To pick that one word, the model runs every one of its ~1,000,000,000,000 parameters — about 2 trillion operations. One token.

× 500 tokens for a normal answer. × 10,000+ when a reasoning model thinks, or an agent works.

Frontier models ~10¹² parameters (dense-equivalent); ~2 FLOPs per parameter per token (Kaplan et al., 2020).

Why it eats power

The arithmetic is cheap.
Moving the weights is not.

Inside an AI accelerator, moving data is ~47% of the energy; the multiplies are ~8%. And every token moves the whole model — a trillion numbers, out of memory, through the chip, and back. That is why racks became 200 kW of memory and interconnect around the GPUs.

Energy per token falls ~10× a year. Tokens per answer rise faster.

MAC: Google TPU, 7 nm, BF16 0.32 pJ (Jouppi et al., 2021). HBM: ~4 pJ/bit at the stack (NVIDIA, MICRO 2017); ~12 pJ/bit effective on an H100 system (2026 calibration) → the real gap is ~400×. Energy split: Dally, NVIDIA, Hot Chips 2023.
